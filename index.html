Questions We Get Asked

The questions that come up in every first conversation — answered directly.

What exactly is "brand architecture" — is this just logo design?
No. Logo design is a deliverable inside a brand system. Brand architecture is the strategic framework upstream of it: how you define your category, your position within it, your naming logic, how you tier and present clinical evidence, what you claim and what you don't, and how your visual language translates across clinical and commercial contexts. We start with positioning before we design anything.
We're pre-clearance / pre-CE mark. Is it too early to work with you?
No — it's actually the right time. Brand decisions made under time pressure at clearance become structural liabilities. Companies that establish positioning, naming, and evidence communication architecture before clearance enter the market with a coherent identity instead of spending 12 months retroactively correcting it. We can work in parallel with your regulatory process without touching it.
How do you work without knowing our specific regulatory situation?
We don't do regulatory work — by design. Our role is brand architecture: positioning, naming, messaging frameworks, visual identity, and evidence communication systems. Everything we produce is reviewed for regulatory alignment by your team. We ensure it's commercially brave and clinically credible. You ensure it's compliant. Those are different jobs requiring different expertise.
What does the "KOL advisory" mean in practice?
Our advisory includes a practicing neurointerventionist active in US, European, and Indian clinical networks. On engagements where clinical credibility is central, they pressure-test messaging for physician-level authenticity — not regulatory approval. The question isn't "does this comply?" — it's "would a skeptical clinician in a peer conversation trust this?" Those require different expertise.
We already have an internal marketing person or agency. Why engage Resonance?
Most internal hires and generalist agencies operate downstream of brand strategy — managing campaigns, social, and production. We operate upstream: defining what you are, who you're for, and how you evidence it across clinical and commercial audiences. If brand architecture hasn't been established at the strategy level, everything downstream is building on an uncertain foundation.
We're a large company. Are you set up to handle us?
We work with strategics selectively, typically on discrete engagements: post-acquisition brand integration, a new pipeline sub-brand, or a product launch positioning sprint. We are not a large agency and don't pretend to be. What we bring to a large client is the strategic conviction and clinical intelligence a large agency typically can't. If you need 50-person account management, we're not the right fit.
How long does an engagement take?
Brand sprints run 3–6 weeks. Full identity systems and launch engagements run 8–16 weeks. Advisory retainers are ongoing. Sales cycles in this market are long by nature — the fastest path to starting is a 30-minute conversation to establish fit.
Who is this NOT for?
Companies looking for performance marketing, social media management, medical writing, or regulatory communications are better served elsewhere — those are not services we offer. Companies that need large-agency infrastructure, manage brand by committee, or treat brand as a compliance document will find our approach a poor fit. We work best with clients who treat brand as a strategic asset and want a small, senior team that operates with conviction.
