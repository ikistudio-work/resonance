Questions We Get Asked

The questions that come up in every first conversation, answered directly.

What exactly is "brand architecture"? Is this just logo design?
Logo design is a deliverable inside a brand system. Brand architecture is the framework upstream: category definition, positioning, naming logic, how you present evidence, what you claim and what you don't. We start there, before design.
We haven't launched yet. Is it too early to work with you?
No, it's the right time. Brand decisions under pressure become structural liabilities. Companies that build positioning early enter the market with a coherent identity. We work alongside your regulatory process, never inside it.
How do you work without knowing our specific regulatory situation?
We don't do regulatory work. Our role is positioning, naming, messaging, and visual identity. Everything we produce is reviewed for compliance by your team. We ensure it's commercially credible. You ensure it's compliant.
What does the "KOL advisory" mean in practice?
Our advisory includes a practicing physician across US, European, and Indian clinical networks. On engagements where clinical credibility is central, they pressure-test messaging for physician-level authenticity, not regulatory approval. The question isn't "does this comply?" It's "would a skeptical clinician trust this in a peer conversation?" Those are different questions.
We already have an internal marketing person or agency. Why engage Resonance?
Most internal hires and agencies operate downstream, managing campaigns and production. We operate upstream: what you are, who you're for, how you communicate it. If that work hasn't been done at the strategy level, everything downstream is built on uncertain ground.
We're a large company. Are you set up to handle us?
We work with strategics selectively on discrete engagements: a post-acquisition integration or a new pipeline sub-brand. We're not a large agency. What we bring is strategic conviction and clinical intelligence a large agency typically can't. If you need 50-person account management, we're not the right fit.
How long does an engagement take?
Brand sprints: 3–6 weeks. Full identity systems: 8–16 weeks. Advisory retainers: ongoing. The fastest path to starting is a 30-minute conversation.
Who is this NOT for?
Companies looking for performance marketing or regulatory work are better served elsewhere. Companies that manage brand by committee or treat it as a compliance document will find us a poor fit.
