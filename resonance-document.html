Resonance: Strategic Brand Partner for Medical Innovation
A statement of intent — the problem, the opportunity, and how we work.

We translate scientific complexity into commercial conviction.

We build brand systems for companies operating at the edge of medicine and market.

The Problem
  • The medical industry has a structural trust problem. Clinical audiences distrust marketing by default. Investors distrust science they can't read. The gap between scientific credibility and commercial clarity is widening.
  • Most companies enter the market with brand systems built under funding pressure, by generalists who have never been at the clinical front line. The pitch deck, the name, and the identity are rarely revisited — until they become liabilities.
  • Companies receive regulatory approval and still cannot articulate what makes them meaningfully different. Names chosen in the seed round become structural problems at Series B. Visual systems fail the dual test of physician credibility and investor confidence.
  • Brand, when treated as a compliance checklist or a cosmetic afterthought, becomes the first thing that erodes trust — before a KOL meeting, before an investor call, before a market entry.
The Opportunity
  • The volume of medical innovation entering the market is at a historic high — more spin-outs, more pipeline brands, more cross-border market entries than any prior generation of medtech and pharma companies.
  • The companies that will win in fundraising, in clinical adoption, and in market expansion are those that can translate their scientific credibility into commercial conviction at every touchpoint. Most cannot.
  • Funds and incubators are increasingly competing on what they offer beyond capital. Portfolio-level brand support is an unmet, high-value differentiator — a systematic program that strengthens the identity of every company in the cohort.
  • Multi-market entry is no longer sequential. Companies are entering the US, EU, and India simultaneously. What earns trust in an FDA context lands differently where KOL endorsement and government hospital relationships dominate. Brand systems must account for this at the architecture level, not as a localisation afterthought.
How We Fit In
  • Resonance is a strategic brand partner built specifically for companies at the intersection of medicine and market. We are not a generalist agency — we are a small, senior collective of practitioners who have worked inside early-stage medtech and pharma firms, building for clinical and investor audiences simultaneously.
  • Our advisory includes a practicing physician active across US, European, and Indian clinical networks. Brand claims are pressure-tested for physician-level authenticity — not just reviewed for compliance. The question is not "does this comply?" It is "would a skeptical clinician trust this in a peer conversation?"
  • We operate upstream — before campaigns, before execution, before trade show materials. We define what a company is, who it is for, and how it evidences that across clinical and commercial audiences. Clients who need execution vendors get referrals.
  • We don't do regulatory work. Our role is brand architecture: positioning, naming, messaging frameworks, and visual identity. Everything we produce is reviewed for regulatory alignment by the client's team.
What We Do
  • Strategy — Brand & Positioning: Category design and positioning, naming and naming architecture, investor narrative alignment, HCP messaging architecture, clinical evidence communication framework.
  • Identity — Visual Brand Systems: Identity designed for clinical and commercial contexts, product and portfolio brand architecture, conference and event identity, scientific communication design, scientific animation and video, web assets and digital brand, SaMD interface and UX brand alignment.
  • Activation — Launch & Market Entry: Market entry brand strategy, investor and roadshow brand materials, conference exhibitor strategy and experience, multi-market brand entry across US, EU, and India.
