Questions We Get Asked
What exactly is "brand architecture"? Is this just logo design?
Logo design is a deliverable inside a brand system. Brand architecture is the framework upstream: category definition, positioning, naming logic, how you present evidence, what you claim and what you don't. We start there, before design.
We haven't launched yet. Is it too early to work with you?
No, it's the right time. Brand decisions under pressure become structural liabilities. Companies that build positioning early enter the market with a coherent identity. We work alongside your regulatory process, never inside it.
How do you work without knowing our regulatory situation?
We don't do regulatory work. Our role is positioning, naming, messaging, and visual identity. Everything we produce is reviewed for compliance by your team. We ensure it's commercially credible. You ensure it's compliant.
What does the clinical advisory mean in practice?
Our advisory includes a practicing physician across US, European, and Indian clinical networks. On relevant engagements, they pressure-test messaging for physician-level authenticity. The question isn't "does this comply?" It's "would a skeptical clinician trust this?" Those are different questions.
We already have an internal marketing person or agency. Why engage Resonance?
Most internal hires and agencies operate downstream, managing campaigns and production. We operate upstream: what you are, who you're for, how you communicate it. If that work hasn't been done at the strategy level, everything downstream is built on uncertain ground.
Who is this NOT for?
Companies looking for performance marketing or regulatory communications are better served elsewhere. Companies that manage brand by committee or treat it as a compliance document will find us a poor fit.
How long does an engagement take?
Brand sprints: 3–6 weeks. Full identity systems: 8–16 weeks. Advisory retainers: ongoing.

We work with companies where brand precision is a commercial necessity, not a cosmetic consideration. If your next raise or market entry depends on how well clinicians and investors read you, that's what we're built for.
